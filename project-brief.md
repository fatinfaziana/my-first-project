# Project Brief

## Project Identity

- Name: **Tuition Service Landing**
- Course: AI Builder Foundations (one evening: Spec, Build, Check; no GitHub, Vercel or deploy tonight)
- One page, no login, no payments, no backend tonight.

## One-Sentence Concept

A single landing page for a tuition service that shows subjects, combo package prices, availability, client feedback and contact details, runs one enquiry form, and adds a product shop showcase plus a teacher AI-marking demo section.

## Target User

Two niches on one page:

1. **Parents and students** looking for tuition: they read the subjects, combo prices, availability and feedback, then send an enquiry.
2. **Product shoppers**: teachers who want to buy marking products, and students who want to check answers or buy completed teacher-made notes from the product showcase.

## User Goal

- Parents/students: understand what tuition is offered and at what price, see when the tutor is available, read trusted feedback, and send one clear enquiry (subject(s), group or personal, package, preferred timing).
- Shoppers: browse the product showcase and understand what each product is.
- Teachers: understand the AI-marking service from its demo section.

## Build Shape

**Content-led site** (single landing page with sample data).

## Shape Confirmation

Confirmed by learner. Reason: the main value is reading and understanding the tuition offer, prices, availability and feedback, with one primary action (the enquiry form). Sample data is display-only; nothing needs to survive a refresh.

## Version-One Success

- All sections show the sample data on screen (profile, subjects and combo prices, availability list, feedback, contact, product showcase, teacher AI-marking demo).
- The enquiry form validates input and shows a clear on-screen success message on submit.
- No backend: the form confirms on screen only tonight.

## Now / Later / Never

### Now

- One responsive landing page with all content sections.
- Subject and combo package price display.
- Availability shown as a list.
- Client feedback/comments display.
- Contact section.
- Product shop showcase (display only).
- Teacher AI-marking demo section (marketing/preview only).
- Enquiry form: subject(s), group or personal, package, preferred timing, with validation and on-screen success message.
- Mobile check and working links.

### Later (needs trainer approval or the paid cohort)

- Login/accounts for clients and teachers.
- Mutual availability agreement (both sides see, choose and agree).
- Real booking engine and scheduling.
- Payments for packages, products and notes.
- A real teacher AI-marking tool (handwriting reading, scanning, keyword marking) with a live AI API.
- Multi-user sync, admin system, uploads.

### Never (tonight)

- No login, payments, database, backend, live API or secret keys.
- No real bookings agreed in-app.
- No real people's names, phone numbers, IC numbers, account numbers, real balances or company names.

## Assumptions

- Payment, login and booking are deferred; the enquiry form stands in for booking tonight.
- The teacher AI-marking tool cannot scan or read handwriting without an AI API, so it is a demo/marketing section only.
- Product showcase is display-only; no cart or checkout tonight.
- Availability is shown as a static sample list, not a live calendar.
- All content uses made-up sample data.

## Proof Target

- On a phone and on desktop: every section renders, all links work, and the enquiry form accepts a valid submission and shows its success message.
